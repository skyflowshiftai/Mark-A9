The Problem

The world is visual. Independence shouldn't depend on sight.

Modern physical environments communicate critical survival and navigational information visually—from approaching traffic and staircase drops to room numbers, banknotes, and safety notices.

The Overload Trap

Existing assistive camera apps describe every visual artifact in view. A stream of continuous noise ("chair, bottle, person, window, door") causes cognitive fatigue and masks real danger.

The Decision Gap

Traditional vision systems stop at object recognition. A visually impaired person doesn't need to know a car exists 15 meters away—they need to know if that car is entering their direct walking path.

Meet MARK

From visual chaos to concise voice clarity.

MARK transforms continuous camera video into context-aware, actionable voice instructions through a 5-stage intelligence loop.

01

SEE

Camera observes the forward corridor at 10-15 frames per second.

02

UNDERSTAND

Local neural vision identifies obstacles, pedestrians, vehicles, and text.

03

PREDICT

Kinematic tracker estimates metric distance, speed, and trajectory overlap.

04

PRIORITIZE

Multi-factor risk engine ranks threats and isolates the single actionable event.

05

SPEAK

Speaks concise, max 5-word guidance. Stays silent when safe.

The Silence Difference

Knowing when NOT to speak is intelligence.

MARK doesn't describe everything it sees. It tells you what you need to know.
